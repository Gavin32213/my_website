Margherita Pizza Co Wine List Notes
Six Ounce Measured Pours ~ Four Glasses are Equal to One Bottle of Wine
RED WINE:
Josh Cellars Cabernet Sauvignon Reserve (CA)$12 glass/$48 Bottle(Josh Cab)
Red Wine. Big and bold dark fruit flavors of blackberry and boysenberry are complemented by rich vanilla and cinnamon spice. The bourbon barrel aging adds richness with a long finish of coconut and black fruit.
14 Hands Merlot (WA)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(14 Hands Merlot)
Red Wine. Washington. Who says well-rounded can’t be fun? This fruit-forward Merlot from 14 Hands brings exhilarating flavors of blackberry, plum, cherry, and mocha. Balanced in style, the wine is soft; food friendly.
La Torre Di Veglia Cabernet Sauvignon (Italy)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(La Torre Cab)
Red Wine. Just the right touch of real, neutral oak barrels. Dark fruits like cassis, blackberry, and plum. Accents include, violet flowers, smoked wood, hints of spice, and a mild tannin that is deep and satisfying.
Donna Laura Ali Sangiovese di Toscana (Italy)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(Ali Toscana Red Blend)
Red Wine. Predominately Sangiovese, which contributes vibrant fruit and structure. A small amount of Cabernet Sauvignon is added to the blend for additional layers of complexity.
Vino Dell’Amicizia Pinot Noir (Veneto, Italy)$12 glass/$48 Bottle(Dell’Amici Pinot Noir)
Red Wine. Means “wine of friendship”. Light-to-medium-bodied, fruity, and approachable Italian red, typically featuring prominent red cherry, raspberry, and subtle earthy, herbal notes. Known for being dry, crisp, and having soft tannins, making it an excellent pairing for pizza, pork, and chicken.
Torre Zambra Montepulciano d’Abruzzo (Maddia, Italy)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(Maddia Montepulciano)
Organic. Clean nose profile shows very fruit scents and jam aromas, such as blackberries and raspberries, with hints of violet and bitter licorices. Supple, warm, well-balanced, firm and pleasantly long-lasting.
Biagio Montepulciano D’Abruzzo (Italy)$9 glass/$36 Bottle(Biagio Montepulciano)
Red Wine. Made from Montepulciano grapes grown in the hilly section of Abruzzo, on terrain sloping down towards the seas, this wine has a vinous and pleasant odor with a delicate bouquet.
Biagio Chianti Reserve (Italy)$12 glass/$48 Bottle(Biagio Chianti)
Red Wine. 85% Sangiovese, 10% Canaiolo, 5% Cabernet Sauvignon. Intense and characteristic bouquet, fruity with a violet fragrance, dry and harmonious with notes of dark chocolate and black cherries.
Justin Cabernet Sauvignon (CA)$55 Bottle(Justin Bottle)
Red Wine. Aromatic with ripe black and red cherry, blackberry and berry fruit, baking spice and sweet tobacco with red and black licorice, oak, cocoa and stable camphor notes. Finish is fresh and moderately long, showing lingering cherry and berry notes with oak and barrel spice nicely supported by fine, balanced tannins.
Canneta Brunello di Montalcino (Italy)$60 Bottle(Canneta Brunello Bottle)
Red Wine. Cranberries and top soil show first on the nose of this wine before violets, rose petals and wild fennel appear. Lively and fresh, the palate offers cherries, strawberries, blackberries, hints of bitter orange, and then more soil and wild herbs. Tannins are dusty and taut, and the acid zings. 92pts. Wine Enthusiast
Amarone della Valpolicella (Veneto, Italy 2014)$70 Bottle(Amarone Bottle)
Red Wine. Rich, bold, smooth dry Italian red blend featuring red currant, raspberry and earthy, aromatic herbs (garlic), along with black plum and cherry. Medium-bodied fine tannins with a clean and lingering finish.
Renato Ratti Nebbiolo (Piedmont, Italy)$80 Bottle(RATTI Bottle)
Red Wine. Medium to Full-Bodied. A benchmark producer delivering classic Nebbiolo character. Red cherry, rose, violet, tobacco, and spice. Structured tannins balanced by vibrant acidity and a persistent finish.
WHITE WINE:
Dipinti Chardonnay (Italy)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(Dipinti Chardonnay)
White Wine. Fresh Italian chardonnay that emphasizes fruit and balance over oak. Golden apple, pear, citrus, and white flowers. Crisp and clean with refreshing acidity.
La Torre Di Veglia Moscato Giallo (Italy)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(Moscato)
Sweet White Wine. Expect a delicately sparkling wine with fresh fruit expressing peach, apricot, honeysuckle, pear, and hints of sage. Some Moscato’s feel like syrup but this one is bright, fresh, and full of real fruit sweetness.
Barone Montalto Pinot Grigio (Sicily)$9 glass/$36 Bottle(Barone Pinot Grigio)
White Wine. A bouquet full of citrus fruits like grapefruit, juicy exotic fruits, and hints of green herbs. In the palate there is a medium acidity that helps give a good structure and lasting finish to the wine.
Santa Margherita Pinot Grigio (Italy)$65 Bottle(Santa Margherita Bottle)
White Wine. Alto Adige, Italy — This dry white wine has a straw yellow color. Its clean, intense aroma and bone-dry taste (with appealing flavor of Golden Delicious Apples) make a Santa Margherita’s Pinot Grigio a wine of great personality and versatility.
Hay Maker Sauvignon Blanc (Marlborough, New Zealand)$10 glass/$40 Bottle(Hay Maker)
White Wine. Lifted notes of grapefruit, guava, and nettles are complimented by concentrated fruit flavors and a lingering finish.
Chloe Prosecco (Italy)$40 Bottle(Chloe Prosecco Bottle)
Prosecco/Sparkling Wine. Chloe Prosecco defines the elegant sparkling style for which this captivating area is known. This wine is bursting with fresh fruit and fine bubbles, with notes of peach, green apple, citrus and white flowers on the nose and palate.
Whispering Angel (Rosé France)$12.50 glass/$50 Bottle(Whispering Angel)
Rosé. Côtes de Provence, France. This is full of mouthwatering flavor with a lovely texture. Lots of light fruit flavors with a clean finish, this wine does well to demonstrate the power of its grape.
Fleurs de Prairie (Rosé France)$12 glass/$48 Bottle(Fleurs de Prairie Rosé)
Rosé. Provence, France. Crafted in the traditional Provencal style with a pale salmon color, delicate flavors of strawberry, rose petals, and herbs, with bright, refreshing acidity.
