Our Story

At Margherita Pizza Co., pizza is more than food. It’s family, tradition, and a passion that has been passed down through generations. Our story begins with Lorenzo Renna, who came to the United States from Sicily at just 19 years old. Arriving in New York without knowing English, Lorenzo brought with him the values he grew up with in Italy: hard work, family, and a love for bringing people together through great food. He began working in his cousin’s pizzeria on Long Island, where he developed the skills and experience that would shape the next several decades of his life. Along the way, Lorenzo met his wife, Diane, and together they built a family and a life centered around food, hospitality, and community. The Renna family spent decades in the pizza business on Long Island, including 25 years in the Hamptons, before deciding it was time for a new chapter. In 2019, the family traded Long Island for the coast of North Carolina and made Surf City home. What began as Lorenzo’s idea for a mobile wood-fired pizza trailer eventually grew into something bigger. In July 2023, Margherita Pizza Co. opened its doors in Surf City, bringing a little piece of the Renna family’s Italian and New York roots to Topsail Island. Today, Margherita Pizza Co. remains a true family business. Our pizzas begin with homemade dough and are topped with carefully selected ingredients before being baked in our authentic wood-fired oven imported from Italy. From San Marzano tomatoes and imported Italian cheeses to fresh, organic and non-GMO ingredients whenever possible, we believe great food starts with caring about what goes on the plate. But what matters to us just as much as the food is the experience. When you walk through our doors, we want you to feel like you’ve been welcomed into our family. Whether you're a Surf City local, visiting Topsail Island for the week, or just passing through, there’s always a place for you at our table. Come enjoy a wood-fired pizza, share a bottle of wine, and experience the food and hospitality that have been part of the Renna family story for decades.
